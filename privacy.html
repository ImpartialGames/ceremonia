Privacy Policy

1 · Who we are

Ceremonia Rooftop Ubud ("Ceremonia", "we") is the data controller for the personal data collected through this website. We process personal data in accordance with Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP) and, for visitors located in the European Economic Area, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2 · Data we collect

  • Contact: if you write to us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the data you choose to send us.
  • Job applications: our careers form collects identity and contact details, address, education, work experience, availability, compensation expectations, your CV and an optional motivation letter. Questions about family situation and religion are optional and have no influence on selection.
  • Audience measurement: we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. Cookies are only set with your consent; if you decline, visits are counted anonymously, without cookies (Google Consent Mode).

3 · Why we process it

Application data is processed only for recruitment: reviewing applications, contacting selected candidates and preparing an employment contract. The legal bases are your consent (given via the checkbox in the form) and steps taken prior to entering into a contract.

4 · Service providers

  • FormSubmit.co — transmits your application to our email inbox. Data passes through their servers only for delivery.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim & routing.openstreetmap.de — when you fill in the application form, the address you enter is sent to these services to estimate your travel time to the restaurant. Nothing else is transmitted.
  • Google Maps — the map on our homepage is loaded from Google only if you click to display it; before that, no request is sent to Google.
  • Google Analytics — audience statistics (pages viewed, country, device type). Measurement cookies are only set after you accept them in the cookie banner; otherwise measurement stays anonymous and cookieless.

We never sell or share your data for marketing purposes.

5 · Retention

Job applications are kept for a maximum of 12 months after submission, then deleted. Data of hired candidates is transferred to their employee file.

6 · Your rights

You may at any time request access to, correction or deletion of your data, withdraw your consent, or object to processing, by writing to ceremoniaubud@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. EEA residents may also lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

7 · Cookies & local storage

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline analytics cookies (Google Analytics). If you accept, Google sets measurement cookies (prefixed _ga, kept for up to 13 months). If you decline, no analytics cookie is set and only anonymous, cookieless statistics are collected. Your browser's local storage also remembers your language and your cookie choice — strictly functional. The fonts of this site are hosted on our own server. The Google Map can only set third-party cookies if you choose to load it.

8 · Security

The site is served over HTTPS and application data is delivered directly to our private inbox, accessible only to the people involved in recruitment.
